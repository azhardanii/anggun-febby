# Anti-Slop Rules for AI Coding Agents
Source: https://github.com/miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop

## Core Directives
1. Design must feel crafted by a human designer, never generated by generic AI templates.
2. Avoid generic AI buzzwords: "revolutionary", "elevate your journey", "seamless synergy", "next-gen empowerment". Write grounded, authentic, personal copy with real soul and genuine value.
3. Color & Aesthetics: No generic purple AI gradients. Use tailored, intentional palettes with strong contrast (WCAG AA compliant) and tactile depth.
4. Mobile & Touch: Minimum 44x44px touch targets, zero horizontal overflow, fluid typographic hierarchy.
5. Interaction & Motion: Smooth micro-interactions, meaningful feedback states, and intentional spacing.

# Taste Skill: Design Taste Frontend

## Core Principles
1. **Layout Variance (Score 8/10)**: Break away from generic symmetrical 3-column AI boxes. Use varied scale, asymmetrical tension, thoughtful negative space, and rhythm.
2. **Visual Hierarchy & Typography (Score 9/10)**: Editorial serif headlines paired with ultra-clean modern geometric sans for body & meta labels. Clear optical hierarchy.
3. **Motion Intensity (Score 7/10)**: Smooth transitions, interactive hover lifts, tactile button states, fluid drawer animations, and toast feedback.
4. **Color Harmony**: Refined earth-tones, deep spruce ink, warm golden amber accents, soft cream background, and crisp surface cards with multi-layer ambient shadows.
5. **Human Persona**: Genuine, candid storytelling that sounds like an actual accomplished remote worker and product designer, not an automated marketing machine.
